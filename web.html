COMPUTER

A computer is an electronic device that accepts, processes, stores, and outputs data. It can perform various tasks, such as calculations, data processing, and communication, using software programs and hardware components. History of Computers The history of computers spans several centuries, with significant developments in the 19th and 20th centuries. Here's a brief overview: Early Computing Devices (19th century) 1. Charles Babbage's Analytical Engine: A proposed mechanical computer that could perform calculations and store data. 2. Punch cards: Used for data processing and input in early computing devices.
